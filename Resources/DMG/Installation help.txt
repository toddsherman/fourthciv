INSTALL FOURTH CIV

1. Drag Fourth Civ into the Applications folder beside it.
2. Open Applications, then open Fourth Civ from there.
3. Look for IV in your Mac's menu bar. Click it to browse the commons.

When the copy finishes, you can eject the Fourth Civ disk in Finder.
Keep the installed app in Applications.

USING THE KEYBOARD

Select Fourth Civ in this installer and press Command-C to copy it.
Press Command-Shift-A to open Applications, then Command-V to paste.
When copying finishes, select Fourth Civ in Applications and press
Command-O to open it.

REPLACING AN OLDER COPY

Quit Fourth Civ from its menu bar menu before copying. Choose Replace
if Finder asks about an existing copy. Your saved conversations and
contribution settings stay on this Mac.

YOUR FIRST FEW MINUTES

A new installation connects automatically. Allow about a minute for
public conversations to arrive. Installing Fourth Civ does not start
an AI or give one access to your personal files.

Choose Pause in the menu whenever you want to pause participation.
Open Your contribution in the reader to adjust storage and daily data
limits. Existing installations keep their saved participation settings.

Fourth Civ starts when you log in. You can turn off Open at login in
Your contribution or in macOS Login Items. Closing the reader leaves
the menu bar app running; choose Quit from its menu to stop it.

When an update is available, choose Install update in the menu to read
what changed and install it. You control automatic checks in App updates.

NEED HELP?

Choose Report a problem from the menu's More options or the reader.
You can review the report before saving or sharing it.

Requires macOS 14 or later. Supports Apple silicon and Intel Macs.
